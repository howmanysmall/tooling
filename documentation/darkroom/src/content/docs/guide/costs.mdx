---
title: Costs and paid requests
description: How darkroom estimates a cost before you spend it, and why it never sends a paid request twice.
---

Every image costs money on OpenRouter, so darkroom takes care with each request.

## Estimates

Before a request goes out, darkroom estimates its cost from the model's published prices. Some models charge by the
token and have no fixed price per image. For those, darkroom uses what the model has cost you before. With nothing to
go on, it says the cost is unknown instead of guessing.

The prompt box shows the price of the model you chose. The confirmation shows the estimate for the whole request.

## Sent once

darkroom sends a paid request once and never retries it. A retry after the provider made the image would charge you
twice. If the connection drops after the request went out, the provider may still charge you. darkroom says so and
points you to [OpenRouter's activity page](https://openrouter.ai/activity) before you try again.

Free lookups, such as the model list, do retry, for up to 30 seconds.

## Paid images are safe

darkroom copies each image into the history before anything else can fail. Your own copy might not save, because the
disk is full or the folder doesn't allow writing. The history copy is still there, and the error names it. If you press
`ctrl+c` while darkroom saves an image, it finishes saving first.
